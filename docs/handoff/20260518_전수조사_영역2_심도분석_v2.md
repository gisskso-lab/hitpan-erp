```
                        sales_delivery_items → stock_ledger(OUT)
                          ↓
                        sales_returns (delivery_id FK)
                          ↓
                        collections (ref_doc_type='sales_delivery' ?)
```

```
purchase_orders → purchase_receipts → tax_invoices(direction=2)
```
