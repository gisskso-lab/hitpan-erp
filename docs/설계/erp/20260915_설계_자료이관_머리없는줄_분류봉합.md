## §3 [조립] 조립·해체① 재고 반영

- DDL 확인(#13·#36): `installer/hitpan_db_clean.sql:3620` stock_ledger — `move_type varchar(10)`(CHECK 없음) · `source_type varchar(30)` 주석에 `bom_production/bom_disassemble/adjust/migration` · UNIQUE `(tenant,source_type,source_id,item_id,move_type,warehouse_id)`. 코드 전역 move_type 값 = `in`/`out` 뿐(`BomService.cs:710·725·882·955`, `StockService.cs:305`).
